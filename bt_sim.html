How to interpret

  • Arrow direction follows value flow: parent transaction → focus transaction → spending transaction.
  • Edges represent known UTXO spends, not proof of common ownership.
  • Bitcoin transactions can have multiple inputs and outputs; change detection is heuristic and not asserted here.
  • Addresses are intentionally not clustered: address reuse and shared transactions can mislead attribution.

Investigation cautions

Graph proximity alone does not identify a person, exchange, illicit activity, or beneficial owner. Treat clustering and attribution as hypotheses requiring corroboration.

Address vs TXID: A wallet address can contain many transactions. The Analyzer now accepts either a 64-character Bitcoin TXID or a Bitcoin wallet address. Ethereum addresses open in Etherscan and are not supported by this Bitcoin-only simulator.
